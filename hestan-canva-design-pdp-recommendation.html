Perfection in the Art of Details

Tri-Ply Titanium-Stainless Construction

Each piece pairs an 18/10 stainless steel cooking surface with Hestan's proprietary aluminum core, fused into a single tri-ply body for fast, even heat distribution and long-term structural stability.

Molecular Titanium NanoLayering

A patented process bonds thousands of ultra-dense titanium layers onto the stainless steel surface, forming a sealed protective shield well beyond what standard stainless finishes offer.

400% Harder Than Standard Stainless

The titanium-reinforced surface resists scratching, pitting, and general wear far better than conventional stainless steel, allowing metal utensils and even steel wool to be used safely.

Non-Porous, Glass-Smooth Surface

The ultra-smooth titanium finish closes off the microscopic pores found in ordinary stainless steel, promoting clean food release and reducing buildup of oils and residue over time.

Inert, Non-Reactive Cooking Surface

Unlike carbon steel or cast iron, the titanium layer won't react with acidic ingredients such as tomatoes or wine, helping preserve flavor and prevent discoloration cook after cook.

High-Heat and Induction Ready

Rated for temperatures up to 1,050°F, / 565ºC, NanoBond performs from stovetop, broiler, oven, open-fire or pizza oven and its construction is compatible with gas, electric, and induction cooktops.

Sealed Rims and Flush Rivets

Fully sealed edges keep moisture from working between layers to prevent corrosion, while flush interior rivets eliminate the crevices where food typically collects, easing cleanup.

Dishwasher-Safe. For Life.

Sealed rims ensure dishwasher safe durability that holds up over time, backed by Hestan's lifetime warranty against wear and delamination.
